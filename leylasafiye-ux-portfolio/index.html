02 / Approach

How I think about research

My psychology studies have made me curious about behaviour. UX research gives that curiosity a practical question: what should a product team do differently?

01 / FRAME THE QUESTION

Understand the problem first

I start by asking what someone is trying to do and what the team actually needs to learn. A feature idea is not the same as a user need.

02 / CHOOSE THE EVIDENCE

Use the right method

Interviews can explain experiences. Surveys can explore patterns. Experiments can test a specific prediction. The question should guide the method.

03 / SUPPORT DECISIONS

Be clear about what is known

I try to separate observations, assumptions and recommendations. If something has not been tested, it should be described as a hypothesis.
